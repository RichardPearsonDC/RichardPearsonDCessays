Richard Pearson DC Has More Thought Insertion - a Threat Pearson heard, "They have one more trick up their sleeve" as Black man with black bookbag passed. Richard Pearson's account of torture in the United States of America is horrific. He is still experiencing torture after more than a year. On July 23, 2026, he had a strange experience after passing a Black man in his 30s near Georgetown. The man inserted a thought in Pearson's mind. "I can tell you precisely where I was. I was coming out from under the Virginia Avenue bridge on the Rock Creek Trail. I am headed south toward the monument. He was a muscular man about 5'11, wearing all black. I try to avoid people, but I couldn't avoid him. Several feet after passing, I heard it, 'They have one more trick up their sleeve." Pearson explains that voice insertion has been a major feature of the campaign since it started last year. Pearson does not know what the man meant. He assumed that it was some kind of threat.  "I don't know why they won't leave me alone! I am a civilian. I am an African American scholar with a PhD who has spent his entire life fighting bullies like them. I worked my way through poverty, depression, and abuse. Just leave me alone!" Pearson strongly desires to leave the United States, unable to live in a country that tortured him horrifically and that is not beholden to its own Constitution and laws. Then, it activated security apparatuses that resulted in his return from two asylum attempts in Iceland (destined for Germany) and Canada. Pearson was tortured in a black site in Canada, then labeled as mentally ill ("does not understand the nature of the proceedings"). Pearson would have either been detained indefinitely in Canada or forced to return to Canada.
