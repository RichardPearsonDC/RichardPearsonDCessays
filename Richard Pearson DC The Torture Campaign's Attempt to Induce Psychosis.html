Richard Pearson DC: The Torture Campaign's Attempt to Induce Psychosis - An Irony A man who has been tortured for more than a year in the United States identifies yet another sinister plot - to induce psychosis "This is all projection! I do not suffer from mental illness or psychosis. The same cannot be said of those tormenting me. This constant need to keep communicating with me is a form of "external validation" - a symptom of psychosis. It's like I am watching a great mass of people in a severe state of psychosis." - Richard Pearson, PhD "Die." - T-shirt message worn by young Black man, facing Pearson's regular spot where he updates his Medium account. … In early June 2026, Richard Pearson continues to experience a torture campaign that has turned into an attempt to induce psychosis. The campaign involves T-shirt messages, a constant stream of stalking, manipulation and compromise of his writing spaces, and the use of devices that trigger clicks with the device in his skull. "Die." Pearson went to his local library in the late evening on June 2, 2026, to publish his daily account of torture in Washington, DC. It is his usual location - a ledge on the outside of the building and in the bushes. It has been compromised. He knew that already. He had hoped that he could quickly post, then be on his way. Standing across the street from him, facing the ledge where he normally sits, a young Black man with all black clothing stood. In big white letters across the T-shirt, it read, "Die." Pearson, who had just rounded the corner, decided on a different location that night. Red and Black On June 3, Pearson goes to his local grocery store. He notices an uptick in red and black. He thought that part of the campaign had subsided, but here it is. Two people posted on the sidewalk. Both in red shirts and black pants. One was at 4th and M St and the other on the opposite, near the driveway. Another was in the park that runs along the metro escalator. "Of course, they were in public areas precisely because they cannot be on private property doing this stuff. They were lingering. Everyone else was walking to where they needed to go unless they were socializing in front of the store. Imagine that. Respecting private property rights while engaging in the torture of a man. Beyond ironic. Increased Street Theater, Difficulty Leaving and Arriving Home While Pearson has varied his paths to and from home, there are only but so many paths that he can take. Via the tunnel behind the Ford House Office Building is one. He has to traverse a parking lot that is under a highway. Today, he decided that he would enter the parking lot from the east end. He takes a rest at a tree. He notices that a man with a green colored bookbag - like the military color - is coming down South Capitol. He heads toward the back of the ballpark. "I know exactly what he is going to do. He is going to go that way, so that he ends up close to me at this end." That is exactly what the man does. Pearson sees the man on his side. With each step, he inches closer. Pearson gets up and goes behind another tree. "All these handheld weapons! I am just so over it." The man makes another lap. Pearson is beginning to understand what is happening - fear-induced paranoia. They know his account of torture. "These psychos don't care. Is this an alien invasion or something?," says Pearson half-seriously. A Black man stood next to a concrete barrier. He eyes Pearson, then eyes the clothing - a shirt and pants - laying on the barrier. He said nothing. Pearson understood the point. He has seen it before. They want to recruit him. Who? He didn't know, but the first occasion happened with a DC Metropolitan Police Department (MPD) officer - same dangling of a blue uniform. Pearson was on his way to sell art. The officer stood beside his car, the uniform on the outside being suspended by a hanger. "First of all, I am a researcher. I have a PhD in research. I have conducted research. My research has been cited. Can't they take a hint? I don't want any role in government, especially federal or local DC. I don't know where their head is at if they think that I would be attracted to any job after I have just been tortured, falsely arrested, assaulted…!" "I get the sense that I am not only being tortured, but being used. They wait for my analytical essays to be published to understand why I wasn't "neutralized". I have written that I don't want to be close to people, so what do they do? Bring people around me. Sick, right? It's all just sick. They have little insight that they represent a grave threat to the national interests of this country. The United States of America - the constitutional democracy - does not torture. In fact, our legal system hardly recognizes that something like this could occur. The democratic mandate is that Richard Pearson, a torture victim, should be entitled to the protections of the US Constitution and US laws." - Richard Pearson, PhD Pearson also sees a Black man on a cellphone in a white shirt and black pants. He pays him no mind really. He is avoiding people and can you blame him. A device in his head that go off with Bluetooth signals. People stalking with devices. Electrocution to the penis - twice. Pearson continues under the bridge. He notices a homeless man pushing a wheelchair which had any number of bags and papers. He first saw him last night. When Pearson passed him, the device in his skull went off. Within a minute or so, it goes off again when a white woman, whom he photographed, appeared from behind the open gate. Seeing this man - a "homeless" person that he'd not seen before - triggered Pearson. He darted across the lot. As soon as he began approaching, he saw a man running from his usual spot under the bridge, along the bushes. It was not the "homeless" man that he'd seen once before. This man had a light complexion and blue shirt. "Now why would he need to run? They want to fuck with my head. That's number one - saturate my environment to induce fear-based paranoia and psychological torture. Beyond sick. Second, I suspect that they are using me for training. That's sick too. A man has been tortured. He survived. Because the US has no domestic remedy for me, all I can do it write. They fundamentally do not grasp the gravity of the situation." Pearson is trying to make his way to way to the other side of the bridge for water. He feels parched after his coffee. He wants a clear path - no zapping him. He waits for the path to clear. He spots another "homeless" man who is lingering. Pearson knows the spot. He has seen that the area had more and more stuff over the last two months, although he has never seen anyone there. "Let's just say that not everyone that appears to be "homeless" is truly homeless. Sometimes, as in my case, they are paid operatives, likely veterans, who participate in a man's torture." Pearson had never seen the man before. Anyone, in fact, near this pile of stuff. Pearson looked inside from afar. The man does not have any intention of leaving. He's placed his left leg on the makeshift seat on the side of the building. "You got to be fucking kidding me with this shit." Pearson knows exactly what is happening. They want to prevent or encumber his path - saturate his environment with stress and fear. Pearson turns around. He decides that he will go the long way around. It will take another 15 minutes. He is also thinking about how dehydrated he feels. "They are messing with a man's health," explains Pearson who has a chronic illness. Pearson decides against taking the long way. He walks toward the tunnel. The man has disappeared in a matter of minutes. Pearson sees him on a grassy hill - enough distance to safely pass. "They are running this game. I am not a deer in a forest, ok?! I am a human being. This is the psychosis that I am talking about - a slavery mindset that someone is not human - a thing, an object of targeting, striped of personhood." Pearson gets his water. He decides to run through an aspect of the campaign involving computer hacking and conditioning. He is talking out loud. He thinks out loud. That is what he does. If it doesn't seem normal to others, he couldn't care less. While seated behind the Ford House Office Building, he notices people doing stunts - coming within his view and suddenly becoming curious about what he is looking at. He has seen that before too. He wrote about it yesterday. They come. They look around. A woman today took a picture. "I cannot tell you want is happening in these people's minds that they think this is OK. No idea. It strongly points to psychosis - a detachment from human emotion, feeling, and empathy that enjoys to cause the suffering of others." A car pulls up. A woman runs out, crosses the street, and begins peeing behind a bush. Pearson leaves. He settles at another set of picnic tables nearby away from the street. Before leaving, he gets the New York license plate. "I didn't know if they had some device or what, but I am out of here," explains Pearson. It might have been a device. Pearson believes that there are devices that can target to bladder. "This was in 2025. I have the date in my notes. I was on my way to sell art at the market. I passed this car and I had a very strong and sudden urge to urinate. I thought that it was bizarre. I had woken that morning - urinated. I should not have to pee again. I ran to take down the license plate. They had pulled into a second spot. When they saw me, they pulled out and headed to the light. I caught up with them, getting their license plate just as they pulled off. The urge subsided to nothing. My instinct was right, but I didn't know why. Now, I know. All these damn weapons." Pearson is at the table, then decides to leave within minutes. "It could have been pinning - the involuntary movement of someone's body." Pearson approached 3rd Street from Virginia Ave. The white man and white woman standing idle in conversation, the older Indian man and woman in close proximity. The Indian woman is initially seen pushing the man in the wheelchair, then Pearson sees the woman passing Potbelly's and walks to the end of 3rd Street to Virginia Ave. He had seen this set up before, just a few days earlier when his computer was hacked at Potbelly's - this white-Indian thing criminal network. This also explains the young wite woman getting out of the car, "Let's put it together. The white man who is driving pulls up in front of me. She jumps out. She goes to pee behind - to condition Pearson who has downed two bottles of water. At the nearest restrooms, a white-Indian group of four appears. The woman passes Potbelly's, then turns back where he pushes the older Indian man." Pearson watches the four people. The middle-aged white man has his hand in his pocket. "I definitely saw it moving. I am cautious, not paranoid. Look, I am tired of being zapped in my head." The "zap" is when Pearson experiences a click from the device in his skull when he comes into contact with Bluetooth signals. The high-powered handheld devices tend to trigger more clicks than lamp posts and cars. They eventually leave. Pearson continues up 3rd Street then makes his way to Starbucks. Eventually, he heads home via the tunnel. An Indian man is standing at the entrance to the tunnel. He is "on the phone." Pearson watches. The man eventually takes several feet toward E Street. He clears the path. Pearson resumes walking. Just as he is a few steps down toward the tunnel, a crowd of white people. Pearson backs out. He eventually leaves. He rests in the parking lot. It hits him. "This is what they want. Me to be fearful. Now, while I have a legitimate reason to be fearful with a device in my skull that responds to Bluetooth signals and on account of all of the other forms of torture, I am on to them. They want to induce paranoia, perhaps psychosis." The irony is not lost. People who are experiencing severe psychosis are trying to induce psychosis in a completely rational and sane African American man who has experienced the most sustained torture on US soil in the modern age. Pearson is not like them. He will never be like them. Almost home, Pearson sees someone who is becoming a fixture in the campaign as well. An older Black man who is sitting on the bleachers. He is smoking. A couple of days earlier when Pearson passed the same spot, the man was smoking and asked Pearson to pick up a cigarette. He declined. This time, the man yells back at Pearson, "Hey!". "It was more of a bark," says Pearson. Pearson ignores him. The man barks again. Pearson looks behind. The man is holding up a cigarette in his hands. "Probably a Black veteran paid by some corporate whatever." Pearson sighs. Homeward bound.
